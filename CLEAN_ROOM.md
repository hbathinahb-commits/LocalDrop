# Clean-room contribution policy

LocalDrop is an independent implementation based only on general product requirements and documented platform behavior.

By contributing, you confirm that your contribution:

- was written by you or comes from a source compatible with Apache-2.0;
- contains no code, assets, secrets, documentation, or non-public knowledge from Tangerine or another proprietary product;
- was not produced by decompiling, disassembling, intercepting, or otherwise reverse-engineering a competitor;
- does not imitate competitor branding or create likely user confusion; and
- identifies any third-party code, model, codec, binary, or dataset and its license.

Maintainers may request provenance notes or reject contributions that make independence difficult to verify.
